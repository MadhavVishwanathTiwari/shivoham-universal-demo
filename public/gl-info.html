Hero backdrop precision probe

Look at the three strips below in a dark room, at the brightness you normally use. You are looking for horizontal contour lines / stepping, not for colour. Which strips band tells us where the hero's wave-shaped seam comes from.

1 — CSS gradient

Bands here → the CSS vignette over the hero is the cause.

2 — WebGL, no dither

Bands here but not in 1 → the canvas buffer is low precision.

3 — WebGL, dithered

If 3 is clean and 2 is not, raising the shader's dither fixes it.
